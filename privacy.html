Privacy

youen.ch is the private, non-commercial website of Youen Aebischer. If you have a question about your data, write to mail@youen.ch.

Visiting the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). To deliver the pages and keep them secure, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

This site sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. Fonts, scripts, photos and 3D models are loaded from youen.ch itself, not from other services.

Contact form

The contact form does not send anything to a server. It only opens your own e-mail app with your message filled in, and you decide whether to send it. If you write to me, I use your name, e-mail address and message only to reply, and I do not pass them on.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time which of your data I have, and ask me to correct or delete it.
